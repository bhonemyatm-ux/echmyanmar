    About ECH Myanmar

    Founded in 2025 as an official partnership between UCSTgi Computer Science and MAEU Aerospace Engineering — building cross-disciplinary excellence through English proficiency and innovative STEM projects.

    Our Journey

    From Chat Sessions to Innovation Hub

    A movement that started with weekly conversations and grew into a cross-university STEM platform.

    Early 2025
    The Spark
    Bhone Myat Min identifies a gap — CS and Aerospace students sharing a common barrier: technical English. The idea for ECH Myanmar is born.
    Mid 2025
    Official Launch
    ECH Myanmar formally established as an official inter-university club, with weekly English Chat Hours running between UCSTgi Taunggyi and MAEU Meiktila.

    Activities

    What We Do

    A range of activities that develop both technical English skills and real-world STEM competencies.

    🗣️

    Weekly English Chat Hours

    Structured conversation sessions focused on technical vocabulary, presentation skills, and professional English for STEM careers.

    🚁

    Drone & AI Projects

    Collaborative builds including drone navigation algorithms, autonomous flight simulations, and AI-powered aerospace data analysis.

    ⚡

    Interdisciplinary Hackathons

    Timed innovation challenges pairing CS and Aerospace students to solve real engineering problems together.

    📊

    Research & Presentations

    Members present research papers, technical demos, and project portfolios in English — building skills for global conferences and industry roles.

    📷

    QR Attendance Tracking

    Smart attendance system with QR member cards, session tracking, and semester-wide engagement reports for both university cohorts.

    🌐

    Career Development

    Resume workshops, mock interviews in English, and networking with industry professionals — preparing members for global STEM careers.
